# Character XI — Unhinged Homepage Experiment

## Project

I'm considering renaming my app, VividGo 2.0, to **Character XI**.

XI is the Roman numeral for 11, a joke referencing Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The app is for AI character chats and roleplaying stories, so I want to poke fun at subscription-based chat services and their business models.

The goal is a funny, irreverent, completely original homepage. It should be entertaining enough that someone might share it just for the jokes.

## Project Locations

- Existing project: `C:\dev\vivid2.0`
- Current homepage: `C:\dev\vivid2.0\vividHomePage`
- New staging folder: `C:\dev\vivid2.0\charXIhome`

All new work must stay inside the staging folder.

## Your Task

Inspect the existing project to find the correct application download link, understand the browser-based version, and verify the application's actual features.

Then create a new numbered HTML homepage in `C:\dev\vivid2.0\charXIhome`.

Inspect the folder first and choose the next available `index*.html` filename. **Never overwrite an existing index file.** This folder is a staging area for experimental homepage designs.

## Design: Forget the Existing Homepage

The existing homepage is too serious.

Use it only to discover technical information and working links. Do not let its layout, colours, typography, section order, marketing language, or visual identity influence the new design.

I want a completely fresh concept, not a redesign or reskin.

Invent your own layout, colours, graphics, typography, and presentation. Make it look nothing like the existing homepage.

Go for an unhinged internet-marketing vibe: absurd corporate announcements, overdramatic warnings, financial jokes, sci-fi references, and unexpected punchlines.

For example, feel free to work in a joke like:

**"These are not the droids you're looking for."**

Perhaps the droids are looking for your credit card. Perhaps the subscription machine has already sent them. Surprise me.

These are examples, not a checklist. Use your own creative judgement. Do not cram every joke or idea into the page.

## What Character XI Offers

The homepage should communicate the following:

- **Free to use:** The app itself is free, although AI models, hosting, or external services may have their own costs.
- **Your data, your control:** Chat data can stay on the user's computer, depending on configuration.
- **Browser and Telegram access:** Users can access the app through a browser or Telegram, depending on the setup.
- **Less networking drama:** Make fun of people trying to enjoy a chat only to encounter an intimidating "Your connection is not private" browser warning, certificate problems, or networking complications.
- **Roleplaying and character chats:** This is what the app is actually for.

A possible joke: "Your characters have enough drama. Your Wi-Fi connection doesn't need a subplot."

Keep technical claims accurate. Browser and Telegram access can avoid some common self-hosting hassles, but do not promise to eliminate every certificate warning or guarantee security and privacy in every configuration.

## Satire and Naming

Do not mention Character.AI directly by name anywhere on the page.

Use phrases such as "your chat provider", "the subscription machine", or "that other chat service".

The Chapter 11 reference is part of the Character XI joke. Do not make unverified claims about another company's finances.

The tone should be cheeky, irreverent, and occasionally ridiculous. Avoid making the page so aggressive or overloaded with jokes that the actual product becomes difficult to understand.

## The Fake Subscription Plans

Include a funny section featuring three fictional plans:

- $5/month
- $10/month
- $20/month

Clearly identify these as satire, not real paid plans. They must not collect payments.

When a visitor clicks a price, reveal a humorous message suggesting they invest that money for their future instead of spending it on another chat subscription.

Make the messages increasingly dramatic for the more expensive plans. Joke about compound growth, future wealth, and paying monthly to maintain a relationship with a fictional vampire.

Keep the investment humour entertaining, without promising guaranteed returns. Make the interactions work on mobile and desktop; do not rely on hover alone.
